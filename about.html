ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Who we are

A community focused on helping young people reach their potential.

Our story

Dlalisa Moyeni Academy is an organisation focused on developing young individuals through sport, education and personal development. Football gives young people a place to learn, challenge themselves and build lasting friendships.

Our vision

To create a positive environment where young individuals can reach their full potential and build a successful future.

Our values

Discipline

Showing commitment and responsibility.

Teamwork

Working together and encouraging one another.

Respect

Respecting teammates, coaches, opponents and the community.

Hard work

Giving our best and learning from every challenge.

Frequently asked questions

Who can enquire about joining?

Parents, guardians and prospective players can contact the academy to ask about age groups, training arrangements and availability.

Can I support the academy?

Yes. You can use the enquiry form to ask about volunteering or sponsorship opportunities.

Where is the academy?

Our listed location is Cnr Mosaic Road and Dekgras Street, Silvertondale, Pretoria.
